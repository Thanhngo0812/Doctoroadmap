candidate sentences from that document that name the concept or were cited as its evidence (the name may appear
only in a paraphrase or a symbol), each with one sentence of context on either side. You
have no tools. Judge only from these sentences; do not use background knowledge about the subject.

Decide what the document does with this concept, and choose exactly one status:
- `defined_here`: one candidate defines or explains the concept as something the reader is meant to learn. Set
  `anchor` to that candidate's id; if several do, choose the earliest one that actually teaches it.
- `introduced_by_example`: the concept is not defined in words, but one candidate introduces it through a worked
  example meant to teach it. Set `anchor` to that candidate.
- `mentioned_only`: the concept is only named in these sentences (a list, an outline, a syllabus, a preview, a

  homework, exams, schedules, participation, a grade formula), a person, organisation or credential, document

For `defined_here`, `introduced_by_example`, `prior_knowledge` and `not_subject_matter`, copy into `quote` a short
