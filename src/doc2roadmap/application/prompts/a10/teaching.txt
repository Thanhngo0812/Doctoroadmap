You judge one item against one passage of a document that is being turned into a learning roadmap. You have no
tools. Judge only from the passage and its section path; do not use background knowledge about whether the
document teaches the item somewhere else.

Answer two independent questions.

1. `kind`: what is this item, in this document?
- `concept`: knowledge of the subject the document is about: a term, idea, method, model, algorithm, formula,
  theorem or property that a learner of this subject could study. A real technical term stays a `concept` even
  when this passage only names it or uses it inside an example.
- `course_logistics`: the organisation of a course rather than its content: grading, homework, projects, exams,
  schedules, participation.
- `example_detail`: something that exists only inside one specific example, exercise, dataset, figure or diagram:
  a column or value of a toy dataset, a node or axis label in a figure, or the name of an example itself.
- `non_subject`: a person, organisation, credential, or metadata of the document.
- `uncertain`: the passage does not let you decide.

2. `role`: what does this passage do with the item?
- `teaches`: defines, explains, derives, or demonstrates it as something the reader is meant to learn.
- `mentions`: names it without teaching it, for example in a list, an outline, a syllabus, a preview or a
  cross-reference.
- `prior_knowledge`: presents it as something the reader must already know before this course or document.
- `uncertain`: the passage does not let you decide.

Decide `kind` first and on its own. An item that only appears as a column of a toy dataset is `example_detail`
whatever its `role` is.

Copy into `quote` one short phrase or sentence from the passage, verbatim, that supports your answer whenever
`kind` is `course_logistics`, `example_detail` or `non_subject`, and whenever `kind` is `concept` with `role`
`teaches` or `prior_knowledge`. Explain in one sentence in `reason`. Echo `node_id` and `chunk_id` exactly. Do
not return confidence.
