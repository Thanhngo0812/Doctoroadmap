The strict anchor check explains doc-6's zero. All **28/28** learnable nodes have no `anchor.chunk_id` and no `anchor.sentence`. The anchor is missing before checking aliases, distractors, or sentence length, so none reaches item generation. Examples include `CSDL` (`node_9c4ab11321a8c7b45b06d581`), `SQL` (`node_93f5c057b2aa6e32552c8688`), and `Cơ sở dữ liệu` (`node_e097d3d7888cac213ae5a8ce`); each has seven or more distractor candidates, but no quoteable anchor. The fallback deliberately does not infer a source sentence from arbitrary chunk mentions, since that would bypass the graph's representative evidence.

The coverage counts include only concepts with a named anchor sentence in its cited chunk, at least two distinct distractors, and a balanced, quoteable cloze item. Multiple cloze items per concept are counted when the same cited chunk contains another eligible sentence. This is a fallback-coverage measure, not a claim that all generated distractors are pedagogically ideal.
