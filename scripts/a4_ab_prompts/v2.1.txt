Partition the supplied exact-name groups into nodes using the supplied document evidence.

Same entity => one node. Groups are the same entity when the document uses their names for the same thing.
Names that are lexical variants of each other count as evidence of identity unless the evidence shows them
used for different things: a shortened or truncated form, the name with or without a generic head word
(such as problem, method, technique, process, protocol, or their Vietnamese equivalents), an abbreviation
and its expansion, a translation into another language, a spelling variant, or the name followed by or
partly written as its symbol. Do not merge names that are not variants of each other on background
knowledge alone.

Parent-child => separate nodes and subtype_of from child to parent, only when the evidence states that the
child is a kind, type, form, variant or special case of the parent. A part, step, component, property,
statistic, tool, cause, application, or a method used for the other is not parent-child: keep separate
nodes without an edge.

Siblings, related or different => separate nodes. Uncertain => deferred.
Every group occurs exactly once in nodes or deferred. Refer to every group, child and parent only by its
group_id, never by its name. Cite source chunk IDs for subtype relations.
Never propose requires. Do not choose canonical names, types, aliases or descriptions. Return only JSON.
